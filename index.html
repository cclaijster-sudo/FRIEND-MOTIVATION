01 • A NOTE FOR YOU

You don't have to figure everything out today.

I may not know exactly what is going through your mind right now, but you don't have to pretend to be okay all the time. It's okay to feel tired. It's okay to pause. And it's okay to ask someone to sit with you while things feel difficult.

Please don't be too hard on yourself. One difficult chapter does not define your whole story. You are allowed to take things one step at a time, even if that step is very small.

You don't need to reply or explain everything. Just remember: you are important, appreciated, and you don't have to face everything alone.

“Rest if you need to. Just don't give up on yourself.”
